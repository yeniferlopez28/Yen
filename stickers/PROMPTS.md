# Prompts para generar los stickers con otra IA

Úsalos en ChatGPT (imágenes), Gemini, Midjourney, Ideogram, etc.
**Adjunta siempre** `sticker-01.png` (tu personaje) y 1–2 imágenes del estilo de referencia,
y escribe antes del prompt: *"Use the attached character as the exact same person, and the attached illustrations only as style reference."*

Están en inglés porque las IAs de imagen entienden mejor así.

---

## 1. Bloque base (pégalo al inicio de CADA prompt)

```
Flat vector sticker illustration in a retro hand-drawn editorial style. Limited palette: deep wine/burgundy (#8B1A3D) for all outlines and line art, warm cream (#FCEFD9) for skin, soft pink (#F7A1B5), orange (#F2864E) and olive green (#6B6B10) as accents. Bold, slightly organic burgundy outlines, simple line-art face (the nose is a single line that continues from one eyebrow), hands drawn with clear separate fingers. Thick irregular cream paper-cut border around the whole figure, like a die-cut sticker. Small pink starbursts and olive four-point sparkles behind. Plain white background, no text, no logos.

Character (keep identical in every image): young woman with a round, soft, full face and a curvy, slightly chubby body. Long, softly wavy, voluminous copper-cinnamon auburn hair (#8A3E1E) with a center part, falling past the shoulders. Thick, defined dark brown eyebrows. Brown almond-shaped eyes with visible lashes. Soft, diffused pink blush on both cheeks (not hard circles). Natural pink lips of normal size (not full). Fair warm skin with NO freckles. Wears a chocolate brown top with a thin lace trim.
```

## 2. Prompts por sticker (van después del bloque base)

**Sorprendida**
```
Bust portrait, facing forward. Surprised expression: eyes wide open with round pupils, eyebrows raised high, small open "O" mouth. Short olive "impact" lines radiating around her head.
```

**Sonrisa grande**
```
Bust portrait, head slightly tilted to one side. Big happy laugh: eyes closed in happy upward curves, wide open smile showing the top teeth. One hand raised near her face waving hello, fingers spread.
```

**Beso**
```
Bust portrait, three-quarter view. Eyes closed, lips puckered in a kiss. One hand next to her mouth blowing a kiss, palm open. Three small hearts (burgundy and pink) floating away from her hand.
```

**Café**
```
Half body, three-quarter view. She sips from a paper to-go coffee cup with a lid, the cup covers her mouth, eyes looking up and to the side with a relaxed, cozy expression. Her hand wraps around the cup with fingers clearly drawn. Over her brown top she wears an oversized cream cardigan with burgundy polka dots. With the other arm she hugs a pink notebook against her chest.
```

**Café con compu**
```
Half body at a desk, three-quarter view. She smiles softly while typing on a pink laptop with a small heart on the lid. A burgundy mug of coffee with steam beside the laptop. Cream cardigan with burgundy polka dots over her brown top.
```

**Caminando**
```
Full body, walking to the right in a confident wide stride, three-quarter view, smiling. Long-sleeve chocolate brown sweater, high-waisted wide-leg flowing pink trousers with burgundy polka dots, burgundy sneakers. The back arm swings behind with an open relaxed hand, the front arm swings forward. Hair moving slightly with the walk.
```

## 3. Consejos
- Si sale distinta la cara o el pelo, pídele: *"Same character as the attached sticker: copper-cinnamon wavy hair, round face, soft blush, no freckles."*
- Para fondo transparente: *"transparent background, PNG"* (o quítalo después con remove.bg / Canva).
- Pide una imagen a la vez; así el personaje se mantiene más igual.
